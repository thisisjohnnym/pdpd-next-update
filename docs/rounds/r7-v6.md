## What changed (stakeholder-facing)

- **Mobile hero opens with a one-shot Tabby fall-in** on the `#f0f0f0` studio ground — clip starts Y-offset and eases to settle as it plays.
- **Soft UI land at media ~1.2s** (~0.8s wall at 1.5× playback) while the fall continues; gallery unlocks when the clip ends.
- **Slide 0 stays on the fall’s end frame** — no swap to the separate a0 still.
- **Gallery scrolls vertically** on mobile (snap stack) with a **vertical tick indicator** bottom-left.
- **Desktop (`lg+`) unchanged** — same v5 two-column split layout.
