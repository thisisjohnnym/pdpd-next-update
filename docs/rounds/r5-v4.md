## What changed (stakeholder-facing)

- Hero gallery leads with A0 product still (not lifestyle video first)
- Five-up Details specs (Height / Width / Depth / Weight / Strap drop)
- Trench portrait slide removed
- Left-aligned Reviews and More like this headings
- Square corners on product cards (More like this, Recently viewed)
- Arrow-free “Write a review” and “View again” links
- Larger 24px UGC section heading
- r5 padding/spacing refresh across modules
- Leather aging restructured: image on top, warm block below
- Brand switcher removed; hero reveal disabled
